# Focus Management

## 1. What is Deep Work
Deep work means focusing fully on one difficult or important task without any distractions.
For example, solving a difficult coding problem without checking your phone is deep work.

## 2. According to author how to do deep work properly?
- Set distraction time: Keep a fixed time to check my phone or use the internet instead of checking it again and again.
- Make deep work a habit: Choose a fixed time every day for focused work, like morning time, and slowly increase the time.
- Plan for tomorrow: Before finishing work, write down the remaining tasks for the next day so the mind feels relaxed.

## 3.How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?
- Check my phone and social media only at a fixed time instead of getting distracted often.
- Do focused work at the same time every day and slowly increase the working time.
- Try to stay comfortable when I feel bored instead of checking my phone immediately.
- Use a tool only when its benefits are really useful and not when it just gives a small benefit.
- Avoid or limit social media if it causes too many distractions and affects my focus.

## 4. What are the dangers of social media, in brief?
1. Too much social media can make me feel lonely and isolated.
2. Seeing perfect lives online can make me feel bad about myself and cause sadness.
3. Social media gives quick rewards that can make it harder for my brain to stay focused.
4. Using social media too much can increase stress and anxiety.
