# Energy Management

## Question 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?

1. Sitting alone and thinking about positive things.
2. Drinking tea and taking a short break.
3. Playing a simple mobile game for some time.
4. Listening to music while sitting quietly.

## Question 2: When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?

1. Facing unexpected problems during my work.
2. Getting confused when several tasks need attention together.
3. Thinking too much about mistakes or unfinished work.
4. Dealing with pressure before an important task or interview.

## Question 3: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

1. Getting excited about starting a new task or project.
2. Feeling motivated and enjoying the work I am doing.
3. Finding it difficult to sit still because of high energy.
4. Feeling confident and looking forward to the result.

## Question 4 : Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief?

1. Sleep is very important for our brain and body.
2. Good sleep helps us learn new things and remember them better.
3. Lack of sleep can affect our health, immunity, and memory.
4. Sleeping at the same time every day helps us get better sleep.
5. A cool and comfortable room can make it easier to sleep.
6. Sleep is not a luxury; it is a basic need for a healthy life.

## Question 5 : What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

1. If I cannot sleep for a long time, get out of bed and do something relaxing, then return when I feel sleepy.
2. I will Avoid sleeping during the day.
3. Avoid coffee before going to bed.
4. Count numbers slowly in my mind before sleeping. It gives my mind something simple to focus on and helps me relax.

## Question 6 : Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise.

1. Feel better: Exercise can improve my mood and focus after working out.
2. Better memory: Exercise helps my brain and improves memory.
3. Protects the brain: Regular exercise can help protect the brain from diseases like Alzheimer's.
4. How much: Try to exercise for at least 30 minutes, 3 to 4 times a week.
5. Easy activities: Walking, climbing stairs, or cleaning the house can also be good exercise.

## Question 7 : What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

1. Exercise 3 to 4 times a week for around 30 minutes.
2. Choose activities that make the heart beat faster.
3. Taking short walks during free time can keep the body active.
4. Using stairs instead of the lift adds more movement to the day.
5. Simple housework like vacuuming can also be a good way to stay active.
